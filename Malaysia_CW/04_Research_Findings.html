6. Early observations to test with the real data

  • Restaurants: price index moved +1.5% yoy while the F&B volume index fell 31.7% yoy in Q2 2020. Price barely changed, so the elasticity will be extreme. Say so.
  • Air passengers: SPPI rose 1.0% quarter on quarter in Q2 2020 while MAHB reported a roughly 96% fall in passenger movements (check whether that is network or Malaysia only) - price up, quantity down: consistent with a supply shock.
  • Palm oil: production in Q2 2020 (5.19m t) was higher than in Q4 2019 (4.67m t) and prices rose strongly by Q4 2021. Check whether Q4 2019 to Q2 2020 shows a supply-shock pattern at all. If not, switch to natural rubber.
  • Stimulus: headline RM530bn but the COVID-19 Fund spent RM38.0bn (2020) and RM37.7bn (2021). Useful for the efficiency judgement in Task 5.
  • Excess mortality estimates range from 7,692 (WHO) to 48,100 (IHME). Use this as a data limitation.
